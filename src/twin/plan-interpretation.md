## Evidence reviewed before modeling

Primary sources: `input/references/blueprints/12.Etaža1.pdf`, page 1, titled **Pritličje**, and `11.Etaža2.pdf`, page 1, titled **Etaža**. Both are single landscape A3-like PDF sheets (841.92004 × 595.32001 pt); no issue date, revision, section, elevation, level height, scale bar, or compass mark is visible. The packet's complete 3508 × 2481 upright images are respectively `ground-floor-page-1.png` and `upper-floor-page-1.png`. These full pages and four readable top/bottom detail crops were inspected, including labels, pale dimension extensions, garage, doors, windows and stairs. The two owner PNGs (`ground floor.png`, `upper floor.png`) were also inspected: the drawings are rotated 180° and cropped, not different revisions. Complete upright pages preserve content missing from the cropped margins. Prepared images inherit the PDF's priority 1 authority. Original PDFs and all four original PNGs are available in the source viewer and retained byte-for-byte in output assets.

No separate handmade sketch exists. All 16 distinct photographs were inspected; `.jpg` is a duplicate, not another observation. Photos are priority 3. NOTES, SOURCES and the owner descriptions are supporting evidence, not corrections to the plans. No remote links or map services were fetched.

## Coordinate and dimension interpretation

All dimensions are interpreted as metres (consistent decimal room widths and full-building spans); this is an assumption because a unit legend is absent. In the model, drawing right is +X, drawing down is +Z, up is +Y. **There is no assumed north.** “Front” is the drawing-top façade with the garage and entrance steps, consistent with the photographed front. Drawing coordinates are retained in `model-data.js` so the trace can be compared directly with each page.

The ground drawing's 12.71 line runs from the left wing's inside façade datum to the main block's right façade datum. The 14.84 line runs down the left wing, from its top inside edge to its bottom inside edge. Neither is the bounding rectangle including balconies and stairs. The main block ends earlier, at the ground sheet's 11.11 depth; the long left wing continues to 14.84. The right access strip is 2.22 wide, starts beside the transverse wall at the front terrace/living division, and ends at the main block's bottom. Its length is marked **8.33 ground / 9.03 upper**. Ground front terrace depth is consequently 11.11 − 8.33 = **2.78**. Upper terrace depth is 11.11 − 9.03 = **2.08**. These edges differ between floors and are retained.

Model calibration uses explicit dimension endpoints, not photographs or apartment areas. Ground inside drawing anchors: X 759→0, 1253→3.70, 2440→12.71 m; Z 362→0, 731→2.78, 1372→7.578093, 1832→11.11, 2325→14.84 m. The intermediate 1372 anchor enforces the explicit 3.47 m clear ground bedroom depth between actual wall faces; the unadjusted uniform trace was 0.052 m too short. Upper anchors: X 840→0, 1300→3.70, 2402→12.71; Z 425→0, 681→2.08, 1800→11.11, 2249→14.84. Linear interpolation between anchors digitizes undimensioned intermediate positions. Slightly different pixel scales correct the sheets' differing image sizes. Floor alignment uses the shared façade, left wing, main divider and rear main wall. Wall edges and opening endpoints are then traced from the appropriate floor, not copied between floors.

The main block's 9.01 width is 12.71 − 3.70; the narrow wing's bottom 3.73 dimension differs by 0.03 from the nominal 3.70 divider datum. Its exterior right wall jogs slightly at the rear, as drawn. Thicknesses vary with traced line weights (~0.09–0.15 m), not an asserted structural specification. The source 3.47 ground bedroom depth, 3.40 upper bedroom width, 2.87 upper App 3 bedroom depth, 1.19 × 1.50 internal recess and other chains are independent cross-checks; pixel traces have small residuals, rather than falsely claiming every annotation is exact.

### Conflicts that cannot be silently reconciled

* On the upper sheet, **12.71 is attached to a horizontal line over the right block**, while **9.01** marks its lower width. The upper terrace also bears **10.00** across a width drawn approximately equal to that block. These endpoints cannot all describe the same width. Ground 12.71 combined frontage, the repeated main-block 9.01, and the aligned visible wall geometry govern the provisional shared footprint. The conflicting upper 12.71 / 10.00 annotations are retained as unresolved, not averaged or treated as owner corrections.
* The wing's bottom 3.73 and room width 3.70 differ slightly, consistent with wall/datum ambiguity; a construction-grade survey is needed. The two sheets' side access lengths (8.33 and 9.03) give different front edges and are modeled separately.
* There are no dates/revisions to establish supersession. All geometry is a documented provisional reconstruction, not an as-built survey.

Footprint: L-shaped enclosed/covered body. The full-width front portion includes the garage at drawing-left and the App 1 terrace/kitchen on the right. The left wing extends beyond the main rear wall. The right access terrace projects 2.22 m beside the main block.

* **Garaža:** long single space at front-left; double outward-swing opening in the drawing-top wall. Solid divider separates it from App 1 and App 5. It is preserved, not converted into accommodation.
* **App 1:** two rear bedrooms side-by-side (drawing-bottom of the main block), doors opening into the large living space immediately above. Bathroom occupies the upper-right corner of the living zone, with access from the circulation strip below. Front-right kitchen is separate from the front terrace/dining area by a wall with a door. The front terrace is open to the façade via the entrance stair opening. A transverse wall separates terrace/kitchen from living/bath, with a broad glazed door and a window in the living portion and a small bathroom window. The living space connects to the right external access strip through a door. Two bedroom windows face drawing-bottom; the front kitchen has a window.
* **App 5:** rear-left wing, below the garage. Bedroom above, kitchen/entry in the middle, bathroom below. Bedroom and bathroom doors connect to the kitchen/entry. Exterior door is in the right wall beside the paired stair system. The drawing depicts a self-contained unit, despite the caretaker-room description.
* **Stairs:** one long straight external flight at drawing-top-right, arriving at the right access strip; one paired/U-return system at the rear notch just right of App 5, with a common remote landing. Both are distinct from the **three front entrance steps** leading to the App 1 terrace. Plan tread marks are visible; exact vertical rises and total construction tread counts are not dimensioned.

The straight flight serves the right strip; the paired one serves App 4. A short access connection at the main rear notch is inferred because the plan flight stops slightly short of the façade line.

## Appearance and assumptions

Photos show pale warm stucco, white shutters, light stone/tile terrace floors, white baluster rails at the front and a low pitched roof with light metal edging. Those materials and simplified balusters are represented. The front façade's photographed arch is appearance evidence, represented on the open ground terrace without changing the plan's support endpoints. Roof ridge location, pitch, attic volume, floor-to-floor **3.05 m**, ground plinth **0.42 m**, wall height **2.78 m**, door height **2.12 m**, window sill/head **0.90/2.12 m**, rail height **0.95 m** and step rises are provisional: there are no sections/elevations. The roof is removable and is explicitly marked inferred. Front columns subdivide an opening only as supported by photos; they do not create new enclosed rooms.

Furniture follows the plan symbols in simplified form; materials follow photographs where possible. It is illustrative, not an inventory. Trees and the small ground pad provide context with assumed positions; no property boundary or true site dimension is claimed. Photographic façade-to-unit associations remain uncertain and never relocate drawing openings.

Owner App 1 68 m², App 2 57 m² and App 3 46 m² are reported only as owner figures, not used as dimensions or certified model areas. App 4 “similar to App 3” conflicts with the longer and differently partitioned drawing. “Caretaker/janitor below App 4” conflicts with the App 5 bedroom/kitchen/bath plan. The separate outside-kitchen description is compatible with the front kitchen but does not prove an additional detached structure. Address 60 appears in both sheet titles and the photographed gate; the context URL's 608 is unresolved. No explicit owner supersession is present.

Analytical overhead comparisons were generated and visually inspected for **both** floors at matching orientation and pixel calibration: `validation/ground-overlay.png` and `validation/upper-overlay.png` (also SVG, with JSON projection data and reproducible generators). Cyan is the actual wall mesh section at 1.10 m above the floor; orange is actual stair tread bounds. The main and wing boundaries, garage, different partitions, bathroom insets, bedroom adjacency, all door/window locations and the upper glazed recess align with the source strokes. Doors remain gaps, not solid walls. Terrace and balcony slab dimensions were checked from actual meshes; low rails are intentionally absent from the 1.10 m wall-section projection. Rear paired flights match their footprint approximately; landing height, tread counts and access transitions are provisional without a section.

**Stair conflict exposed by comparison:** the straight stair is drawn ending at the side-strip front edge, which lies at Z=2.78 ground and Z=2.08 upper. A single flight cannot have both endpoints. The provisional 3D flight follows the upper arrival (run 4.03 m, start Z=−1.95) to avoid passing through the upper deck. In the ground comparison it therefore finishes about 0.70 m before the ground drawing's endpoint. The paired system's left flight has an approximately 0.1 m lateral trace residual. These are explicit unresolved cross-sheet/vertical-access discrepancies, not claimed exact matches. The two systems and their three-flight arrangement are retained. Exact upper entry landing connections, especially the App 4 doorway beside the return stair, need a measured section; they are unverified.

**Browser limitation:** system Chromium could not start: the snap wrapper failed confinement; direct Chromium, after resolving bundled library paths, failed its required Unix socket `shutdown` call with “Operation not permitted.” Consequently no WebGL screenshot, browser interaction test or mobile screenshot was possible in this environment. The PNGs above are 2D analytical projections of constructed meshes, **not browser screenshots**. Their visual review establishes plan trace alignment only, not a visually verified browser rendering. A separate dependency-free CPU perspective projection (`validation/perspective-preview.png`, generated by `render-snapshot.mjs`) was also inspected to review the constructed envelope and roof; this is likewise not a WebGL screenshot and does not test the interface. Runtime controls, graphics fallback, on-demand rendering and responsive styles were implemented and statically reviewed; browser behavior remains unverified. The viewer offers a calibrated live drawing overlay for further review in a graphics-enabled browser.

Remaining limitations: conflicting upper width annotations; nominal wall thickness/dimension datum residuals; inferred heights, roof and vertical stair design; no certified area or true site boundary; uncertain photo/unit associations; browser rendering and interactive usability not verified here. This is a usable provisional model with disclosed discrepancies, not a completed as-built survey.
